# System logos

The "Logos" system pictures draw each system's tile from its logo here
(`<folder>.svg`), in the colours of the theme in use (`backend/logo_tiles.py`).

The logos come from **[Monochrome Gaming Logos](https://github.com/HVR88/Monochrome-Gaming-Logos)
by HVR88**, recreations made to identify the systems. They're the trade marks
of their brand owners and aren't part of RetroManager: they're not in git, and
`tools/fetch_system_logos.py` fetches them. A system with no logo here keeps its
drawn tile.
