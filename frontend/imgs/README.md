# System pictures (optional)

The `*.jpg` pictures in this folder, one per system, are **not RetroManager's
own** and are not needed: RetroManager draws a tile for every system
(`frontend/tiles/`) and shows those unless these are chosen (Tools › Settings ›
Appearance, or the Console's settings for one browser or phone).

They are modified copies of the system pictures in **Switch-Light**, a theme
for EmulationStation by **Lilbud**
(<https://github.com/lilbud/es-theme-switch-light>), which is based on the
Switch Theme for LaunchBox by **Grila**. The theme's `theme.xml` gives its
licence as **Creative Commons CC BY-NC-SA** (Attribution, NonCommercial,
ShareAlike): they may be passed on and changed with this credit, not used
commercially, and changed copies carry the same licence. These copies were
changed by this installation's owner and are offered under that licence.

`template.psd` is the template these copies were made with: this installation's
owner's own file, after Lilbud's design, and so under the same licence.

The licence covers Lilbud's artwork. The consoles' names and logos shown in the
pictures belong to their makers.

A copy of RetroManager meant for commercial use, or one that would rather not
carry them, can delete every `*.jpg` here: nothing else changes.
